The five upgrades — in priority order

1 · CM Manager that works like a human

Your #1 problem

The learning layer exists and is wired — but it has recorded zero lessons. It only captures a correction when you send one as a revise order through dispatch. Every other way you say "I don't like this" is lost, so Era keeps repeating the same misses. That's why the content quality never climbs.

  • Capture every correction, not just typed ones. Any "no, do X instead" during a CM run — spoken, Telegram, or a rejected asset — should record as a lesson in your exact words.
  • Show Era its own past mistakes. Feed the last N lessons + your best approved examples into every brief as "here's what Karim rejected before" so it self-corrects up front.
  • A visual QC gate before you ever see it. Era critiques its own render against the reel-art-director checklist (lived-in vs empty, shot variety, lighting) and re-renders weak shots automatically — you only review what already passed its own bar.
  • Learn what you approve, not only what you reject — so good patterns compound.
Why this first: it's your stated biggest pain, and the fix is wiring, not a rebuild — the machinery is already there, it's just not being fed.

2 · True 24/7 on Gemini

Reliability

Most of the 24/7 machinery already exists: era_supervisor.py auto-restarts Era on any crash (with a crash-loop brake), and session_manager.py saves the Gemini session so a restart resumes instead of forgetting. The real ceiling is Gemini's free-tier quota — the 1011 errors. No single free key survives 24/7.

  • Rotate across several free Gemini keys. When one hits quota, Era switches to the next automatically — multiplying your free daily allowance.
  • Fall back to local Ollama when all keys are spent. Era already ships text_ollama.py / vision_ollama.py — wire them as the automatic safety net so Era stays awake (in a lighter mode) instead of going silent.
  • Confirm the watchdog is actually installed as a Windows Scheduled Task (register_era_supervisor.ps1) so it starts on boot, every boot.
  • One status line you can check — "awake, key 2 of 4, 1 quota trip today" — via Telegram.
Why: a free key alone cannot do 24/7. Key-rotation + local fallback is the only cost-free way to make "always on" real.
